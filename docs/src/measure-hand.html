What to print

Grips
Hands

Photo

Palm up, fingers straight and slightly apart, a ruler beside the fingers at palm height, phone parallel to the table. You can also drop or paste the photo.

Points

Click two ruler marks far apart, then these four points on every finger, as the box over the photo asks. Where a joint has two creases, click between them.

All points Click one to place it again.
    Show measurements

    Photon: the segments and how far each base crease sits along the hand place the roller (Height, Depth); the end joint to tip sets the blocker (To_Blocker). Spark: the base and middle joint creases place the roller (Height). All relative to the pinky, in mm.
