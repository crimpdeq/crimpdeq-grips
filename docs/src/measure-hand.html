What to print

Grips

Photon trains the FDP, the deep flexor that bends the end joint: a claw grip with the fingertips pulling on the rollers and a blocker at each end joint that keeps it from becoming a crimp. Fitted to your hand from a photo.

Spark trains the FDS, the superficial flexor that bends the middle joint: the fingers hang over larger free-spinning rollers with the end joint on top. Its standard roller positions fit most hands; fitting it is optional.

Hands

Points

  • First two ruler marks far apart beside the fingers, e.g. 0 and 10 cm or 0 and 4 in: camera tilt changes the scale across the photo, so avoid marks next to the palm.
  • Then the four points of every finger, as the bar under the photo asks. Where a joint has two creases, click between them.

It must match the marks you click: a 1 cm span entered as 10 cm makes every value ten times too large.
