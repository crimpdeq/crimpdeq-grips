# Parts

How to put together a printed Spark grip. The pictures show the right-hand grip at the
model's defaults; a left-hand grip is its mirror image and goes together the same way.

You need the 14 printed parts, a cord for the anchor, a mallet or hammer with a small wood block,
and optionally an inert lubricant such as Super Lube.

The ring, middle and index walls look alike: label them as you take them off the plate (the
slicer's object list names every part). Each wall's cradle matches its roller.

| Part | | How to recognize it |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | ![Frame](images/frame.png) | The largest part, with the anchor's sloped bar engraved `Crimpdeq Spark` and the cord tube |
| Ring wall | ![Ring wall](images/ring_wall.png) | Cradle for the 38 mm roller |
| Middle wall | ![Middle wall](images/middle_wall.png) | Cradle for the 40 mm roller |
| Index wall | ![Index wall](images/index_wall.png) | Cradle for the 30 mm roller |
| End wall | ![End wall](images/end_wall.png) | Thin plate engraved `crimpdeq.com` |
| Pinky roller | ![Pinky roller](images/pinky_roller.png) | 22 mm, the smallest and longest |
| Ring roller | ![Ring roller](images/ring_roller.png) | 38 mm |
| Middle roller | ![Middle roller](images/middle_roller.png) | 40 mm, the largest |
| Index roller | ![Index roller](images/index_roller.png) | 30 mm |
| Pinky pin | ![Pinky pin](images/pinky_pin.png) | The longest pin, about 36 mm |
| Ring pin | ![Ring pin](images/ring_pin.png) | About 25 mm, 1 mm shorter than the middle and index pins |
| Middle and index pins (2) | ![Pin](images/middle_pin.png) | About 26 mm, interchangeable |
| Post | ![Post](images/post.png) | Long square bar, about 105 mm, one corner chamfered |

The illustrations come from the OpenSCAD model; regenerate them with `docs/illustrations/render.sh`.
